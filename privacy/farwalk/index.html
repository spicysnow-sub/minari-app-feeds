Farwalk Privacy Policy

Health data

Farwalk reads your walking and running distance (and, for display only, your step count) from Apple Health to move you along the journeys you create. This data stays on your device. Farwalk never sends Health data to the developer or to any third party, never uses it for advertising, and never writes anything to Apple Health.

Places you enter

The starting point and destination of a journey (names and coordinates) are stored on your device. Your current location is used only once, when you choose “Use my location” to set a starting point, and is not tracked afterwards or uploaded.

Shared journeys (iCloud)

If you choose to walk a journey together with someone, Farwalk stores that journey in your iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit, and shares it only with the person you invite. What is shared: the journey’s name, its start and end points, and each participant’s total distance walked. What is not shared: your step history, your location, or any Health data beyond the total distance. The developer runs no server of their own and cannot read shared journeys. Sharing requires an iCloud account signed in on your iPhone; nothing else is created.

Anonymous usage

To improve the app, Farwalk sends a small number of anonymous events to the developer’s server, such as “a journey was started”, “a journey was completed”, “the plans screen was shown”, and “a share was created”, together with the app version, OS version, language, a random identifier generated on the device (it disappears when you delete the app), and the country of connection (IP addresses are not stored).

The following are never sent: place names or coordinates, distances or step counts, Health data, your location, names, email addresses, contacts, the advertising identifier (IDFA) or the device identifier (IDFV), or IP addresses. These events are not used for advertising and are not shared with data brokers; no third-party analytics SDK is included.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings › “Share anonymous usage”. Turning it off also deletes any events not yet sent.

In-app purchases

Purchases are made through Apple’s App Store. The developer does not receive your name or payment details.
