Airroll Privacy Policy

Airroll does not collect any data.

Your photos

Airroll ("the app") reads your photo library only to show your photos and videos one at a time, to analyze them on your device, and to make the changes you choose (delete, favorite, add to an album, turn a Live Photo into a still). Photos, videos and the results of the analysis never leave your device. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

On-device analysis

Suggestions such as blurry shots, receipts, documents and similar photos are made with Apple's Vision framework on your device. No image, score or result is uploaded.

What the app stores

Your choices (which photos you kept or marked for deletion), your progress, your settings and the space-freed counters are stored only on your device, inside the app. Deleting the app removes them.

What the app does not do

  • No account and no sign-in
  • No analytics, no telemetry and no crash-reporting service of its own
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier (IDFA) and no tracking
  • No third-party SDKs
  • The app itself makes no network connections. If some originals are stored only in iCloud, the Photos framework of iOS may download them so you can see them in full size; that is handled by iOS, not by the app.

Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with developers only if you have chosen to share them in your device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements). These are collected by Apple, not by the app.
