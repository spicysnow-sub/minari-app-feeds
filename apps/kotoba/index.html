Kotoba

Quotes, lines from books, your own words — one of them, always in sight.

Kotoba keeps the words you want to remember, and pins one of them to your Home Screen. Not a feed to scroll. One phrase, chosen by you, waiting where you already look.

View on the App Store

How it is paid for

  • Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • No ads.
  • No notifications. It never asks for your attention.
  • No account, and nothing to sign up for.
  • Twenty phrases and one widget stay free forever.

What it does

  • Save a phrase with its source and tags
  • Pin one to a Home Screen widget, and choose which one
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • A colour for each card, so a phrase is recognisable at a glance
  • Starts with a few sample phrases so it is never empty
  • iOS 17 or later

Privacy

The phrases you save are stored on your device. See the privacy policy.
