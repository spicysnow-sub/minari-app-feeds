Slowdial

Time as an amount. No ads, ever.

日本語

Slowdial shows time as an amount: a colored disc that shrinks as time passes. Children (and grown-ups) can see "how much is left" without reading a clock. Slowdial never shows an ad — not before the timer, not after it. It is safe to hand to a child or to put in front of a class.

Pricing, plainly

  • Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • No ads, ever. No account.
  • The disc, presets, end sound and vibration, and the Lock Screen disc stay free.

What it does

  • Full-screen shrinking disc
  • Presets from 1 minute to 1 hour, or any time you like
  • Whole-circle or 60-minute dial. The time you pick fills the whole circle — 5 minutes or 45, the disc starts full
  • End sound and vibration, even with the screen locked
  • Lock Screen and Dynamic Island disc — keeps shrinking without opening the app
  • Pro: Classroom mode — just the disc, full screen. Taps don't stop it. Hide the numbers. Pick the color
  • Pro: Sequences — "Clean up 5 min → Wash hands 3 min → Snack", with a picture and a word for each step
  • Pro: Controls (iOS 18 or later) — start your favorite timer from Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action Button
  • Pro: Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • English and Japanese · iOS 17 or later

Questions

Is there a visual timer with no ads?
Yes. Slowdial never shows an ad — not before the timer, not after it. The disc, presets, end sound and vibration, and the Lock Screen disc are free, and Pro is a one-time purchase. No subscription. No account.

Is it safe to use with children or in a classroom?
Yes. There are no ads, ever, so it is safe to hand to a child or to put in front of a class. Pro's Classroom mode shows just the disc, full screen, and taps don't stop it.

If I pick 5 minutes, does the disc use only part of the circle?
No. The time you pick fills the whole circle. Pick 5 minutes or 45 — the disc starts full either way. Prefer the classic 60-minute dial? Switch with one tap.

Does it show on the Lock Screen?
Yes. The disc keeps shrinking on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, without opening the app. When time is up you get a sound and a vibration, even with the screen locked. It is free.

Where are my timers stored?
Only on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.
