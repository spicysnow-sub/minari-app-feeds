Yotei

Today's events on your Home Screen — a calendar widget. No subscription.

日本語

Yotei is a calendar widget for iPhone with no subscription. It reads the calendars already on your phone — iCloud, Google, Outlook — and shows what's next, right where you already look. Read-only. Nothing leaves your phone.

Pricing, plainly

  • Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • No ads. No account.
  • Read-only: Yotei never edits your events.
  • A Small widget with today's events stays free forever.

What it does

  • Today's next events in a Small widget, updated as the day goes on; the current event is marked NOW
  • Works with every calendar account already set up on your iPhone
  • Pro: Medium (today + tomorrow) and Large (up to 7 days), choose which calendars show, hide all-day events
  • Available in English and Japanese
  • iOS 17 or later

Questions people ask

Is there an iPhone calendar widget without a subscription or ads?
Yes. Yotei's Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and there are no ads. A Small widget with today's events is free forever.

Does it work with Google Calendar or Outlook?
Yes. Yotei reads whatever calendars are set up on your iPhone through the system calendar — iCloud, Google, Outlook, Exchange — so there is nothing to connect.

Can I show only my work calendar?
Yes, with Pro you choose which calendars appear in the widget, and you can hide all-day events like birthdays and holidays.

Can the widget show tomorrow or the whole week?
With Pro, the Medium widget shows today and tomorrow side by side and the Large widget shows up to 7 days ahead.

Does Yotei change or upload my events?
No. Calendar access is read-only, and your events are never sent anywhere.
