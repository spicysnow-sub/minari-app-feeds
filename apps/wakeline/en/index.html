Wakeline

A slip never erases the days you built.

日本語

Wakeline counts the days since you quit — alcohol, smoking, social media and more — and puts them on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. If you log a slip, your total stays; only the current streak starts again.

Pricing

  • Pro is a one-time purchase. Not a subscription.
  • No ads, no account.
  • One tracker, the Small widget and Lock Screen widgets are free forever.

What it does

  • Total days, current streak, longest streak and the next milestone (7, 30, 60, 100, 150, 200, 365 days…)
  • Log a slip and keep your total — only the streak restarts
  • Hard moment: a ten-minute timer and your reason. Each time you make it through is counted
  • A letter to your future self that opens on day 30 (free)
  • Small widget and Lock Screen widgets (free)
  • Pro: your photo fills the widget and the home card
  • Pro: your own words to read in a hard moment
  • Pro: private title (shows only “Days”)
  • Pro: any number of trackers, the Medium widget and letters to day 100 or a year
  • English and Japanese · iOS 17 or later

FAQ

Is there a sober counter without a subscription?
Yes. Wakeline is free for one tracker and the Small widget, and Pro is a one-time purchase.

Do I go back to zero after a slip?
No. “Log a slip” restarts only your current streak. The total you built stays.

I don’t want people to see what I’m quitting.
Turn on Pro’s Private title and your widgets and Lock Screen show only “Days”.

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen → tap Edit (or +) → search “Wakeline” → choose a size and tap Add Widget. The app’s home also shows a preview under “Put it on your Home Screen”.

How do I change or delete a tracker?
Tap the title on the home screen to open Edit tracker. “Delete this tracker” is at the bottom.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. What you are quitting, your notes and letters are never uploaded.

Wakeline is not a medical app. If things stay hard, please reach out to a professional or a support line.

Privacy

Your trackers, notes, letters and photos stay on your device. Privacy policy.
