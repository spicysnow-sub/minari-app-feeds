Datto

You don’t have to open it to see how many days are left.

Datto is a quiet countdown that puts the days until your important dates right on your Home Screen. There is no habit to keep. Put it there once, and every glance tells you how long is left.

View on the App Store

How it is paid for

  • Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • No ads.
  • No account, and nothing to sign up for.
  • One widget stays free forever, along with up to three events.

What it does

  • Counts down the days to the dates you care about
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • Repeating events — yearly or monthly, rolling over on their own
  • Milestone reminders (100 days, the day before, the day itself) — always silent
  • Hours and minutes as the day gets close
  • iCloud sync — the same days on every device signed in to the same Apple Account
  • 12 curated colours, plus any custom colour; text colour adjusts to stay readable
  • iOS 17 or later

Questions people ask

Is there an iPhone countdown widget without a subscription?
Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. One widget and up to three events stay free forever. No ads, no account.

How do I see the days until a date on my Home Screen?
Add the date in Datto, then add the Datto widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen. The days left are shown there without opening the app.

Can it count down to a birthday or anniversary every year?
Yes — events can repeat yearly or monthly and roll over on their own.

Does it sync between iPhone and iPad?
With iCloud sync on, the same days appear on every device signed in to the same Apple Account.

Privacy

What you enter is stored on your device. With iCloud sync on, it lives only in your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.
