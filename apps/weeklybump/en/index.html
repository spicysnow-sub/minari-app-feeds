Petalweek

Your pregnancy week, one petal a month.

日本語 · English · ไทย · 한국어 · Português · Español

Add your due date once, and your Home Screen shows how far along you are and how many days to go, updated every midnight. One petal fills in each month; ten petals make forty weeks.

View on the App Store

How to use

  1. Open Petalweek and enter your due date (or the first day of your last period). For twins or triplets, choose the number of babies and add a nickname for each.
  2. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and add Petalweek. The Small widget is free.
  3. The widget updates every midnight. This month's petal fills in a little more each week.

More to enjoy

Choose a flower color
In Settings → Appearance, choose Rose or Peony. Your widgets follow.

Bump album (Pro)
One photo a week, all forty in one place, with a slideshow.

Milestone countdowns (Pro)
Show days to go until maternity leave, a baby shower or your own milestones on your widget.

Close tracking
Settings → “Close tracking” removes your due date, photos and milestones and clears your widgets.

Pricing

  • Free: your week, days to go and petals in the app, the Small widget, and flower colors. No time limit.
  • Pro (one-time purchase, not a subscription): Medium, Large and Lock Screen widgets, the bump album, milestones, and a weekly note.
  • No ads. No account.

FAQ

Is it a subscription?
No. Petalweek is free to download, and Pro is a single one-time purchase. Nothing renews.

Does it work for twins or triplets?
Yes. Choose the number of babies and add a nickname for each. There's one week count and one due date.

Is it medical advice?
No. Petalweek only shows your week and days to go from your due date.

How do I contact you?
In Petalweek, go to Settings → “Contact / report a problem” to send an email. The app version, iOS version and iPhone model are filled in (never your due date, nicknames or photos).

Requirements

  • iPhone (iOS 17 or later)
  • English, Japanese, Thai, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil) and Spanish

Privacy

Your due date, nicknames and photos stay on your device. The app sends only anonymous usage records, which you can turn off in Settings. Privacy Policy
