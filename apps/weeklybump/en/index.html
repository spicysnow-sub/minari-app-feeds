Weekly Bump

Your pregnancy week, one ring at a time. No subscription.

日本語

Weekly Bump is a pregnancy week widget for iPhone. Add your due date once, and your Home Screen shows how far along you are and how many days to go — updated every midnight. Every week adds one ring: forty rings, forty weeks.

View on the App Store

Pricing, plainly

  • Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • No ads. No account.
  • The Small widget stays free forever.

What it does

  • Your week (weeks + days) and days to go, on a free Small widget
  • Enter your due date, or the first day of your last period
  • One ring for every week — a quiet picture of time, not a comparison to fruit
  • Close tracking anytime with one tap; the widgets clear and you are not asked why
  • Pro: Medium and Large widgets, Lock Screen widgets, a quiet note the morning each new week begins
  • Available in English and Japanese
  • iOS 17 or later

Questions people ask

Is there a pregnancy widget for iPhone without a subscription?
Yes. Weekly Bump's Small widget is free forever, and Pro is a one-time purchase — not a subscription. No ads.

Does it need an account or upload my due date?
No. There is no account. Your due date stays on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere.

Can I see my pregnancy week on the Lock Screen?
Yes, with Pro: rectangular, circular and inline Lock Screen widgets.

Is it medical advice?
No. Weekly Bump shows your week and days to go from your due date. It gives no medical advice.

Privacy

Your due date stays on your device. Privacy policy.
