Airroll

Swipe photo cleaner. No subscription, no ads, no data collection.

Airroll is a swipe photo cleaner with no subscription, no ads and no data collection. Pay once, and your photos never leave your device.

How it works

  • Swipe left to mark a photo for deletion, right to keep it, up to add it to Favorites or an album.
  • Nothing is deleted on the spot. At the end, one tap and a single iOS confirmation clears everything you marked. Undo your last swipe at any time.
  • Your progress is saved. Finished months get a Done mark. “Today’s 50” lets you clear a little every day.
  • On-device AI groups similar shots and bursts and suggests the best one, and lists likely deletes first: blurry shots, screenshots, receipts and documents.
  • See the size of every photo and video, and how much space you have freed. Turn Live Photos into stills to save space.
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Works on iPhone, iPad and Mac with Apple silicon.

Questions

Is there a subscription?
No. Airroll is a one-time purchase with no in-app purchases and no ads.

Does Airroll upload my photos?
No. Airroll collects no data and makes no network connections. All analysis runs on your device. See the privacy policy.

Where do deleted photos go?
To “Recently Deleted” in the Photos app, where they stay for 30 days. Empty it there to get the space back right away.

Will I see the same photos again?
No. Photos you kept are not shown again unless you choose “Go through kept photos again” in Settings.
