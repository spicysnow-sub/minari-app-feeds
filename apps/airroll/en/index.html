Airroll

Swipe photo cleaner. No subscription, no ads, no data collection.

Airroll is a swipe photo cleaner with no subscription, no ads and no data collection. Pay once, and your photos never leave your device.

How it works

  • Tap Start. Your photos come one at a time, in the order you took them (oldest or newest first). You always pick up where you stopped.
  • Swipe left to mark a photo for deletion, right to keep it, up to add it to Favorites. Tap an album under the photo to file it there in one tap. Undo your last swipe at any time.
  • Nothing is deleted on the spot. At the end, one tap and a single iOS confirmation clears everything you marked.
  • Similar shots: bursts and near-duplicates are shown side by side. Tap the ones you want to keep; the rest go to the bin.
  • Smart picks: blurry shots, receipts, documents and screenshots are listed first, found on your device.
  • More ways to sort: largest first, videos, screenshots, selfies, and turning Live Photos into stills to save space.

Questions

Is there a subscription?
No. Airroll is a one-time purchase with no in-app purchases and no ads.

Does Airroll upload my photos?
No. Airroll collects no data and makes no network connections. All analysis runs on your device.

Where do deleted photos go?
To “Recently Deleted” in the Photos app, where they stay for 30 days. Empty it there to get the space back right away.

Will I see the same photos again?
No. Photos you kept are not shown again. If you change your mind about one you marked for deletion, take it back out of the bin.

Video has no sound?
Use the speaker button on the video card. Music you are playing keeps going while videos play.
