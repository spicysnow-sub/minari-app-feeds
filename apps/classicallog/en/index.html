ClassicalLog

A journal for the classical concerts you attend — programme, seat, notes, and the counts that come from them.

日本語

ClassicalLog keeps every concert you have heard, piece by piece, and turns the pile into something you can read: how many times you have heard a symphony, how much of a composer's cycle is left, which block of which hall you liked. Logging is free with no limit. Records stay on your device.

Pricing, plainly

  • Logging concerts, the ledger, seat notes and export are free, with no limit.
  • No ads. No account.
  • Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase that never renews. It adds on-sale-date alerts for as many ensembles as you like, and a reminder the day before. Subscriptions renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends; manage them in your Apple account settings.

What it does

  • Pick the concert from a catalogue — or type it. The catalogue is updated weekly with programmes from the orchestras' own sites
  • Count the music — the programme is stored piece by piece, so the ledger counts works and cycles
  • Seats from your own record — keep the block you sat in and what it was like, hall by hall; next time you buy for that hall you decide from your own notes
  • Export — JSON and CSV, whenever you like
  • iPhone, iOS 17 or later. English and Japanese

Only concerts and programmes are taken — facts, never text or images. Each concert links back to its source. On-sale dates are read straight from the box office where it publishes them (Spektrix venues in the UK, the Boston Symphony Orchestra, Calgary, NZSO, Japan). Where an orchestra does not, the app marks it “No on-sale dates yet” rather than promising an alert it cannot send.

Questions people ask

Is there an iPhone app for keeping a record of classical concerts I've attended?
ClassicalLog is that: performance, ensemble, programme piece by piece, seat block and notes. Free with no limit; export to JSON or CSV.

Can it count how many times I've heard a symphony?
Yes — and how much of a composer's cycle is left. The programme is stored against a works master.

Can I pick the concert from a list instead of typing it?
Yes, from the catalogue above, updated weekly.

Does it tell me when tickets go on sale?
Yes, where the box office publishes them: UK venues and orchestras selling through Spektrix (LSO and the Barbican, Symphony Hall Birmingham, RSNO, Usher Hall, Bristol Beacon, Saffron Hall, Oxford Philharmonic, Bournemouth SO, Opera North, Scottish Opera), the Boston Symphony Orchestra, the Calgary Philharmonic, the NZSO, and Japanese orchestras and halls. Elsewhere the app says “No on-sale dates yet” next to the name.

Where are my records stored? Do I need an account?
On your device. No account. Anonymous usage counts only, switchable off in Settings.
