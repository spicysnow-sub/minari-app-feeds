Stickwell

One note, where you look every time you pick up your phone.

日本語

Stickwell puts a sticky note on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. Each widget shows the note you choose, so one note never overwrites another. The widgets are free, Pro is a one-time purchase, and your notes stay on your iPhone.

Pricing, plainly

  • Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • No ads. No account.
  • Notes and all Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets stay free.

What it does

  • Unlimited notes; Home Screen widgets in Small, Medium and Large; Lock Screen widgets
  • Touch and hold a widget to choose which note it shows
  • Pro: Shrink to fit — on-device AI shortens a long text into a few lines; numbers, times and names stay as written
  • Pro: Show at the right time — a note appears only during the time you set
  • Pro: Photo to sticky — the text in a photo becomes a note
  • Pro: Pin for now — one note on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island for a few hours
  • Pro: Siri and Shortcuts — create, add to, replace or read a note; pass it to other apps such as ChatGPT
  • Pro: eight colors, five fonts, and StandBy
  • AI features need Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later, iOS 26) · English and Japanese · iOS 17 or later

Questions

Is there a sticky note widget without a subscription?
Yes. Stickwell's notes and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets are free, and Pro is a one-time purchase.

Can each widget show a different note?
Yes. Touch and hold a widget and choose the note it shows. Adding a new note never changes the others.

Can I put a note on the Lock Screen?
Yes. Lock Screen widgets are free. With Pro you can also pin a note to the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island for a few hours.

Does the AI send my notes anywhere?
No. Shrink to fit uses Apple Intelligence on your iPhone (iPhone 15 Pro or later, iOS 26). Your notes are not uploaded.

Privacy

Your notes stay on your device. Privacy policy.
