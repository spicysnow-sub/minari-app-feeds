OPT Days

Unemployment days are counted, not estimated.

Your OPT unemployment count is the one number that can end your F-1 status, and it keeps climbing even while you are outside the US. OPT Days keeps that number on your Lock Screen, so you are never guessing how much of the 90 or 150 days you have used.

View on the App Store

How it is paid for

  • The limit alert is free. You are told when you are approaching the 90-day or 150-day limit without paying anything.
  • No ads.
  • No account, and nothing to sign up for.
  • Pro is either a yearly subscription, which starts with a one-week free trial, or a one-time Lifetime purchase that never renews. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends; you can cancel it in your Apple Account settings.
  • Pro unlocks the Lock Screen widgets, deadline alerts, the gap simulator, unlimited employers, and dated export.

What it does

  • Counts the 90-day and 150-day limits the way SEVP counts them
  • Merges overlapping jobs, so a period of two employers is never double-counted
  • Tracks the deadlines nobody tells you about: filing windows, EAD expiry, the six-month travel signature, I-983 validation reports, the 60-day grace period
  • A gap simulator, so you can see what turning down an offer would cost before you decide
  • Lock Screen widgets, so the number is there without opening anything
  • iPhone, iOS 17 or later

Privacy

Your dates are stored on your iPhone and are not sent anywhere. There is no account. See the privacy policy.

What this app is not

OPT Days is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with USCIS, SEVP, DHS, or your school, and it is not legal advice. Your DSO and SEVIS are the authoritative sources for your record — always confirm with them before you act.
