Leafday

Which plants need water today? Tap the drop. No subscription.

日本語

Leafday shows which of your plants need water today, right on your Home Screen. Tap the drop on the widget and it's done. No plant identification, no AI diagnosis, no subscription — just your pots and how many days until each is thirsty.

View on the App Store

Pricing, plainly

  • Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • No ads. No account.
  • The Small widget stays free forever.

What it does

  • Today's plant and a water button on the free Small widget
  • As many plants as you like, with optional photos
  • Suggested intervals for common houseplants — always a guide: check the soil first
  • Pro: Medium widget (next three days), Large widget (every plant and days to go), Lock Screen widgets, a morning reminder
  • Available in English and Japanese
  • iOS 17 or later

Questions people ask

Is there a plant watering reminder without a subscription?
Yes. Leafday's Small widget and unlimited plants are free, and Pro is a one-time purchase.

Can I mark a plant as watered without opening the app?
Yes. Tap the drop on the Home Screen widget.

Does it identify plants?
No. Leafday deliberately skips identification so it can work without a server or a subscription.

Where are my plants and photos stored?
On your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Privacy

Your plants and photos stay on your device. Privacy policy.
