Museday

A famous painting on your Home Screen, every day.

Museday shows one public-domain masterpiece in a Home Screen widget each day, changing at midnight. Open the app to see the title, the artist, the year, the museum and the museum's own text about the work.

What you get

  • 365 paintings built into the app — it works offline, with no account and no ads
  • Home Screen widgets (Small, Medium, Large) and Lock Screen widgets
  • The museum's text for each painting, in English, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil) and Simplified Chinese
  • Favorites and the last 7 days, free

Pro (one-time purchase, not a subscription)

  • Medium and Large widgets
  • Widgets that show a different painting — your favorites, a period or a museum
  • Every past day, with filters by period, museum and artist

Where the paintings come from

Images are public-domain (CC0) releases from the Art Institute of Chicago and The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Texts are by the Art Institute of Chicago (CC BY 4.0); translations are by Museday and are marked as such in the app.

Privacy

Your favorites and settings stay on your iPhone. The app sends only anonymous usage records, which you can turn off. Privacy Policy
