Museday

A masterpiece on your Home Screen, every morning.

日本語 · English · Español · Português · 简体中文

Museday is an art-of-the-day app for iPhone. Each night at midnight, a new painting takes its place in your Home Screen widget — a quiet museum visit every time you unlock your phone.

View on the App Store

How to use

  1. Open Museday to see today’s painting. Tap the painting to read the museum’s own story of the work.
  2. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap “Edit” → “Add Widget”, and add Museday.
  3. The widget changes to the next painting every day at midnight. The mat around the painting shifts a little through morning, day, evening and night.

Getting more out of it

Keep your favorites
Tap ♡ on a painting’s page to keep it. Find them again under “Favorites” on the Today screen.

See it in person
Tap the museum’s name on a painting’s page to open the museum in Maps. Paintings aren’t always on display, so check the museum’s website before you visit.

A line of the story on Large (Pro)
The Large widget adds the first line of the painting’s story under its title, artist and year.

Choose what a widget shows (Pro)
Touch and hold the widget, open “Edit Widget”, and under “Show” pick your favorites, a period or a museum. Each widget can show different paintings.

Every past day, with filters (Pro)
Open any day’s painting from “Past days” on the Today screen, and filter by period, museum and artist.

Pricing

  • Free: today’s painting in a Small widget, Lock Screen widgets, the museum text, favorites, the last 7 days, and See it in person. No time limit.
  • Pro (one-time purchase, not a subscription): Medium and Large widgets, widgets that show your favorites or a chosen period or museum, and every past day with filters.
  • No ads. No account needed.

FAQ

Is Museday a subscription?
No. Museday is free to download, and Pro is a single one-time purchase. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

Does Museday show ads?
No. There are no ads anywhere in the app, in the free version or in Pro.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No account is needed. All 365 paintings and their texts are built into the app, so the widget keeps changing every day even offline.

My Medium or Large widget says “Museday Pro”.
Medium and Large widgets are part of Pro. In the free version they show the painting only, with “Museday Pro” where the title would be. Tap it to see what Pro includes.

Pro isn’t working on my other iPhone.
Check that the App Store on that iPhone is signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with, then tap “Restore Purchases” in Museday’s Settings. Right after buying, it can take a little while to show up.

Where do the paintings come from?
The images are public-domain (CC0) releases from the Art Institute of Chicago and The Metropolitan Museum of Art. The texts are by the Art Institute of Chicago (CC BY 4.0); translations are by Museday.

How do I contact you?
In Museday, go to Settings → “Contact / report a problem” to send an email. The app version, iOS version and iPhone model are filled in for you.

Requirements

  • iPhone (iOS 17 or later)
  • English, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Simplified Chinese

Privacy

Your favorites and settings stay on your iPhone. Museday never records which paintings you look at. It sends only anonymous usage records, which you can turn off in Settings. Privacy Policy
