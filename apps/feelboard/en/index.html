Feelboard

Tap your mood on the widget. No subscription.

日本語

Feelboard is a mood tracker that lives on your Home Screen. Tap one of five moods right on the widget — no need to open the app. Every day becomes one colored square, and over a year the squares become one picture of how your year felt, from warm to cool.

View on the App Store

Pricing, plainly

  • Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • No ads. No account.
  • The Small widget stays free forever.

What it does

  • Tap a mood on the free Small widget; log as often as you like
  • A one-line note, and this month's calendar in the app
  • Colors on one warm-to-cool scale, so a year reads at a glance
  • Pro: Medium widget with this week, a Large widget with this month's calendar and your year as one strip, the in-app year view, tags and what lifts your mood, weekday and monthly trends, Face ID lock, your own moods (up to ten), an evening reminder
  • Available in English and Japanese
  • iOS 17 or later

Questions people ask

Is there a mood tracker widget I can tap without opening the app?
Yes. Feelboard's Small widget is interactive and free: tap a mood and it is logged.

Is there a Year in Pixels app without a subscription?
Yes. Feelboard Pro is a one-time purchase and includes the Large Year in Pixels widget and the in-app year view.

Where are my moods and notes stored?
On your iPhone. There is no account and nothing you log is uploaded.

Is it a mental-health service?
No. Feelboard is a simple personal log, not a medical or mental-health service.

Privacy

Your moods and notes stay on your device. Privacy policy.
