Farwalk

From your home to someone else’s — the real distance, walked.

Pick any two places on Earth. Farwalk measures the real straight-line distance between them and moves you along it with the walking distance Apple Health already records. Your Lock Screen shows how far is left. Walk one journey with someone from both ends, and meet in the middle.

How it is paid for

  • Everything is a one-time purchase. No subscription.
  • No ads. No account. Sharing uses your iCloud, not a developer server.
  • Free walks one journey at a time, any distance, and keeps every finished journey.
  • One more journey $2.99 · Pro $9.99 (unlimited journeys at once, arrival forecast, postcards, widget themes) · Pro for two $14.99.

What it does

  • Any start and any destination: an address, a city, a friend’s house
  • Curated destinations to collect: Seven Wonders, World Heritage, capitals, peaks, islands, Japan
  • Uses your Health walking + running distance directly — no step-to-distance guessing
  • Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets
  • Walk together: share a journey, walk from both ends, meet in the middle

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 17 or later
  • Apple Health with walking distance (an Apple Watch improves accuracy but is not required)
  • Sharing a journey needs iCloud on both iPhones
