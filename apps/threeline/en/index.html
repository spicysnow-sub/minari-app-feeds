Threeline

Three things today. Check them off on the widget. No subscription.

日本語

Threeline is a to-do widget for iPhone with no subscription. Write the three things that matter today, and tap them off right on your Home Screen — no need to open the app. Three lines, not thirty.

Pricing, plainly

  • Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • No ads. No account.
  • Today's three things and the interactive Small widget stay free forever.

What it does

  • Three lines for today; a new, empty day every morning
  • Tap a circle on the Home Screen widget to check it off — right on the widget
  • Carry over yesterday's unfinished things, or let them go
  • Pro: Medium and Large widgets, eight weeks of history and streaks, a morning reminder at the time you choose
  • Available in English and Japanese
  • iOS 17 or later

Questions people ask

Is there a to-do widget for iPhone that I can tap without opening the app?
Yes. Threeline's Home Screen widget is interactive: tap a circle and the item is checked off right there. The Small widget is free.

Is there a simple to-do app without a subscription?
Yes. Threeline's Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Today's three things and the Small widget are free forever, with no ads and no account.

Why only three things?
Because three is what gets done. Threeline deliberately has three lines a day — not a backlog. Unfinished things can be carried over to tomorrow or let go.

Does it track streaks?
Your current streak shows on the main screen for free. Pro adds Medium and Large widgets with the streak, and eight weeks of history.

Where is my list stored?
On your device. Nothing you write is uploaded anywhere.
