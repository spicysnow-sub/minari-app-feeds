Bloomlist

A bucket list that blooms: someday → a date → a countdown → the photos from the day you did it.

Write down what you want to do — a big dream or a small want. Each one grows through three stages: Want, Planned, Done.

This season

Bloomlist opens on what fits right now: things whose best season has come, plans coming up soon, and goals for your age. Type “cherry blossoms”, “fireworks”, “autumn leaves” or “ski”, and it suggests the right months — for your hemisphere.

A date and a countdown

Pick a date and it goes into your calendar as an all-day event, with a quiet countdown in the app.

The photos from that day

When you mark something Done, Bloomlist shows the photos you took that day. Keep the ones you like with it, and watch your Bloomed album fill up.

Questions

Is Bloomlist a subscription?
No. Bloomlist is free to download, and Pro is a single one-time purchase. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

Does Bloomlist show ads?
No. There are no ads anywhere in the app, in the free version or in Pro.

Do I need an account?
No. Your list stays on your device, and in your own iCloud only if you turn on sync.

How does it find the photos from the day I did something?
When you mark an item Done, it looks in your photo library on your device for photos taken that day and lets you pick. Photos are never copied or uploaded; Bloomlist keeps only a reference.

What is free and what is Pro?
Free: unlimited items, all three stages, the share sheet, season suggestions, calendar dates, countdowns, the Small widget and photos for your first 3 done items. Pro: photo albums for every done item, target ages, Medium/Large/Lock Screen widgets and iCloud sync.

Privacy

No account. Your list stays on your device. Photos stay in your photo library and are never uploaded. Bloomlist sends only anonymous usage records, which you can turn off in Settings. Privacy Policy
