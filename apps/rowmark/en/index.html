Rowmark

+1 row from the Lock Screen. Hands stay on the needles.

日本語

Rowmark counts rows without making you open the app. Put the counter on your Lock Screen, tap +1 on a Home Screen widget, or press the Action Button. Every project keeps its own count, target and repeat.

Pricing, plainly

  • Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • No ads. No account.
  • The Small widget stays free forever.

What it does

  • A free Small widget with +1 and −1
  • As many projects as you like — each with its own rows, optional target and an optional "every N rows" repeat
  • Pro: a Lock Screen counter with +1 and −1 (also in the Dynamic Island and StandBy)
  • Pro: a "+1 row" control for Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action Button (iOS 18 or later; Action Button on iPhone 15 Pro and later)
  • Pro: Medium and Large widgets and Lock Screen widgets
  • iOS limits the Lock Screen counter to about 8 hours at a time — start it again from the app for long sessions
  • No patterns, PDFs or community — just the count
  • Available in English and Japanese · iOS 17 or later

Questions people ask

Is there a row counter without a subscription?
Yes. Rowmark's Small widget with +1 and unlimited projects are free, and Pro is a one-time purchase.

Can I count rows without opening the app?
Yes. Tap +1 on the Home Screen widget, or with Pro on the Lock Screen counter or the Action Button.

Does it work with the phone locked?
With Pro, the Lock Screen counter has +1 and −1 buttons you can press while the iPhone stays locked.

Where are my projects stored?
On your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Privacy

Your projects and counts stay on your device. Privacy policy.
