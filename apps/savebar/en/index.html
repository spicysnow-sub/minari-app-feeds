Savebar

Your savings progress, every time you pick up your phone.

日本語

Savebar puts your savings goals on your Home Screen and Lock Screen as a progress bar. Tap + on the widget when you put money aside. No bank connection and no account — you add the amounts yourself, and they never leave your iPhone.

Pricing, plainly

  • Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • No ads. No account.
  • The Small widget stays free forever.

What it does

  • A free Small widget with a progress bar and a one-tap button that shows the amount it adds (like +$10)
  • Add a photo of what you're saving for — the Small widget shows it big
  • Paste a product link and the photo and name fill in for you
  • Add any amount in the app, or tap one of the amounts you use often
  • Each goal has a target and an optional deadline
  • With a deadline: days left and how much per day
  • Pro: as many goals as you like
  • Pro: Medium and Large widgets with several goals — two amount buttons per goal
  • Pro: a different currency for each goal
  • Pro: Lock Screen widgets — a progress ring and what's left to go
  • Any currency; no conversion, no bank linking, no budgeting
  • Available in English, Japanese and Spanish · iOS 17 or later

Questions people ask

Is there a savings goal tracker without a subscription?
Yes. Savebar's Small widget and one goal are free, and Pro — more goals and bigger widgets — is a one-time purchase.

Does it connect to my bank?
No. You add amounts yourself, so there are no logins and nothing to link.

Can I add money without opening the app?
Yes. Tap the amount button (like +$10) on the Home Screen widget. To add a different amount, open the app and tap “+ Add”.

How do I change the amount on the widget button?
Open the goal, tap “…” › Edit, and change “Widget amounts”. Medium and Large widgets can show a second amount.

How do I delete a goal?
Touch and hold the goal in the list and tap Delete. You can undo it for a few seconds.

A store link didn't fill in the photo.
Some stores don't let apps read their pages. Choose a photo from your library instead.

Where are my goals and amounts stored?
On your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Privacy

Your goals, amounts and photos stay on your device. Privacy policy.
